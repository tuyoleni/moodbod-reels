An estimated $90 million gone in 75 minutes.

On Prime Day, 16 July 2018, Amazon's site hit problems within minutes of the sale starting, and shoppers saw error pages with dog pictures. One Click Retail estimated the outage at around 75 minutes and about $1.2 million a minute, roughly $90 million in lost sales (other estimates ran from $72M to $99M, and nobody can pin it exactly). Amazon said shoppers still bought over 100 million products.

Amazon can absorb a bad hour. A small business in Namibia can't. If your website, booking link or WhatsApp order flow breaks when customers are ready to pay, they buy somewhere else.
