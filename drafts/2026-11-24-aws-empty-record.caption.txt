One empty DNS record took Snapchat, Venmo and Fortnite offline.

On 20 October 2025, a fault in Amazon Web Services' US-EAST-1 region began late on 19 October (11:48 PM PDT), according to The Register's postmortem coverage. A race condition in DynamoDB's automated DNS system left an empty record, and AWS said all services were back to normal by 3:01 PM PT on 20 October. Engadget listed apps like Snapchat, Venmo, Fortnite, Reddit and Zoom among those hit.
