J.Crew's website kept showing one screen on Black Friday 2018: "Hang on a sec."

On and off for about five hours, shoppers couldn't sign in or finish their orders during the 50% off sale. Retail aggregator LovetheSales.com estimated it hit around 323,000 shoppers and cost about $775,000 in sales. J.Crew extended the deal to Monday. A small business in Windhoek usually doesn't get that second chance: the customer just orders from the shop next door.
