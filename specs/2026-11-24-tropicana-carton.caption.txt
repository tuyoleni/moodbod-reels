A new carton cost Tropicana about $30 million in two months.

In January 2009, Tropicana (owned by PepsiCo) redesigned its Pure Premium orange juice carton. The orange with the straw was gone, replaced by a white carton with a glass of juice. Shoppers complained they could not recognise their juice. According to Ad Age, unit sales fell 20% between 1 January and 22 February, roughly $33 million in dollar sales. On 23 February Tropicana announced the old carton was coming back.
