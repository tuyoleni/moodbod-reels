Blockbuster laughed at a $50 million offer.

In 2000, according to Fortune, the Netflix founders tried to sell their startup to Blockbuster for $50 million and were laughed out of the room. Blockbuster had more than 9,000 stores by 2004 and made $800 million (16% of revenue) from late fees alone in 2000. It filed for bankruptcy in 2010, and Fortune says it is now down to a single store.
