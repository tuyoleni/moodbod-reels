They paid up to $12,780 for paradise. They got a cheese sandwich.

Fyre Festival promised a luxury weekend in the Bahamas in April 2017, with models, yachts and top acts in the ads. Guests arrived to half-built tents, feral dogs and limp cheese sandwiches. The organiser, Billy McFarland, was later sentenced to six years in prison, after at least 80 investors lost more than $24 million (according to BuzzFeed News).
